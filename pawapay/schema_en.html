adLyn: collection and payout flow through PawaPay

adLyn, a product of SAWALI SMART SYSTEMS (PawaPay account holder, Burkina Faso) · a single PawaPay account · partner mobile phone shops

1End customer

Orders a phone, an accessory or a repair on a partner shop's online storefront. Mobile Money is offered only if the shop's KYC file has been approved.

➜selects "Mobile Money"

2adLyn platform

Re-checks KYC, creates the order, generates a unique depositId and stores it with the shop ID, merchant code and order number before calling PawaPay.

➜Payment Page API
(depositId, amount,
reason = order no. + shop,
metadata = shop ID, merchant code)

3PawaPay

Hosted payment page: the customer confirms on their phone (Orange Money, Moov Money…). Funds land in the PawaPay account of SAWALI SMART SYSTEMS.

➜callback + deposit
status check

4Reconciliation

The confirmed deposit is matched to the order and the shop; the shop sees the payment in its account (and only its own payments).

6Partner shop

Receives the net amount on the Mobile Money or bank account recorded in its file. Payout receipt available in its account.

⬅payout
(traceable reference)

5Payout by adLyn

Monthly payout, at the end of each month. Statement per shop: collected / already paid out / still due. Each payout lists the depositIds it covers (a deposit can only be paid out once), the fees withheld and the transaction reference.

Shop identification for every transaction
depositId ↔ shop_id · merchant_code · order number: sent to PawaPay in the metadata of every deposit.
Customer name, amount and date: kept by adLyn and available on request (no personal data in the metadata).
Other flows (adLyn's own revenue)
Shops pay their platform subscription and, where applicable, their SMS service: these deposits are adLyn's own revenue and are never paid out.
